Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · updated 9 November 2026 · Bahasa Indonesia

This page explains what data is involved when you visit littlebhre.com and play the Little Bhre games (Pesta Makan, Mandi Busa, Kereta Tutu, Pesawat Bhre and future games). In short: we do not collect children's data, we show no ads and we use no trackers.

1. Inside the games

  • Level progress, purchases and a device code are stored only in your phone's browser (local storage). This data is never sent to our server.
  • The device code is a random string that ties an unlock code to your phone. It contains no name, phone number or other personal data.
  • The games never ask for a name, email, photo, location, microphone or camera.
  • Clearing your browser data removes progress and purchases on that phone.

2. When you buy

  • Payment is made with QRIS (DANA), an Indonesian payment system; it is processed by DANA and your bank or e-wallet under their own privacy policies. We only see the amount and the sender name shown by DANA. International payment is not available yet.
  • WhatsApp: after paying, you send us a message with your order code. We then receive your WhatsApp number, profile name and message. We use them only to check the payment, send your unlock code and help if you change phones.
  • We keep the chat and receipt as long as needed for that service and delete them on request. We never sell or share this data and never use it for advertising.

3. On the website

  • No cookies, ads or third-party analytics. Fonts and images are served from our own server. Your language choice (ID/EN) is remembered in your browser only.
  • Like every website, our host (Hostinger) automatically logs technical data such as IP address, time and browser type, for security and reliability.
  • Links to YouTube and WhatsApp take you to those services, where their own privacy policies apply.

4. Children (COPPA and GDPR)

The games are made for children aged 2–5 playing with or under the supervision of a parent. Purchase screens sit behind a parental gate, and purchases and WhatsApp chats are done by adults. We do not ask for, and do not knowingly collect, personal information from children. This is in line with children's privacy rules such as COPPA (United States) and the GDPR (European Union).

5. Your rights

You may ask for a copy, correction or deletion of the data we hold from a purchase chat, under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27/2022) and, where it applies to you, the GDPR. Contact us on WhatsApp below.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the latest version and its effective date will appear on this page.
