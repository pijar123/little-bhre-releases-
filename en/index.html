Bhre is 2 years 6 months old. He loves watching and playing, like any child his age.

We wanted the best shows and games for him: ones that teach something, are safe to copy, and have no ads. So we made them ourselves.

That is how the Little Bhre cartoon series and our games for little ones began. We show everything to Bhre first. If he gets it right away and laughs, we share it.

How to buy extra levels

  1. Play the free part until your child loves it (Morning Garden in Pesta Makan, levels 1–3 in Mandi Busa, Kereta Tutu and Pesawat Bhre).
  2. Tap a locked world or level and answer the grown-ups-only question.
  3. Pay with QRIS (Indonesian payment only for now), then tap Send to WhatsApp and attach the receipt.
  4. We reply with an unlock code.
  5. Copy our reply into the game, or tap the link in it. Everything opens right away.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a game to my home screen?

Android: open the game in Chrome and tap 📲 Install (or menu ⋮ → Install app). iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. The game icon appears like an app and works without internet.

I changed phones or cleared my browser data

Unlock codes are tied to one phone. Send your old receipt to our WhatsApp and we will make a new code for free.

What data do you collect?

None from the games: no accounts, ads or trackers. The QRIS payment and WhatsApp chat are only used to send your code.
